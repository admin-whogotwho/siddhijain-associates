GST Export & Refund
Calculator

Work through the LUT route or the payment-of-IGST route for a preliminary refund estimate.

Core LUT formula: Zero-rated turnover × Net ITC ÷ Adjusted Total Turnover. The actual refund is subject to the statutory definition of Net ITC, exclusions, documentary conditions, Rule 89 and applicable restrictions. The payment route shown is the IGST actually paid and is not a determination of refund eligibility.
Zero-rated supplies

Understand the two principal refund routes.

Exports and supplies to specified Special Economic Zone recipients are zero-rated supplies under section 16 of the IGST Act, subject to the statutory conditions. The calculator helps with a preliminary comparison of the LUT route and the payment-of-IGST route.

For an unutilised-ITC refund calculation, the statutory formula and the definitions of Net ITC, zero-rated turnover and adjusted total turnover matter. The actual refund can also depend on documentation, restrictions, realisation requirements and the current rules and notifications.

Practical checklist

Before filing a refund claim

Confirm zero-rated status

Verify that the transaction qualifies as export or another supply covered by section 16 of the IGST Act.

Choose the route

Review whether the transaction is being undertaken under LUT without payment of IGST or under a permitted payment-of-IGST route.

Net ITC

Use the statutory definition and exclude credits that are not eligible for the refund calculation.

Turnover figures

Ensure zero-rated turnover and adjusted total turnover are determined consistently with the applicable refund rules.

Documents

Check invoices, shipping or export documents, returns, electronic ledgers and other evidence required for the claim.

Current law

Refund provisions and procedures can change. Verify the current Act, Rules, notifications and circulars before filing.
