Corporate Tools & Calculators

Useful answers before
you make the decision.

Interactive working tools for corporate law, transaction, valuation and FEMA questions. Each tool states its legal basis and is intended for preliminary assessment, not a substitute for transaction-specific professional advice.

Transaction valuation

Pre / Post-money Valuation

Mathematical model. No statutory assumption is embedded in the formula.

Formula: Post-money valuation = Investment ÷ investor ownership %. Pre-money valuation = Post-money valuation − Investment.
Companies Act · MCA · SEBI LODR

ROC & Listed-Entity Compliance Calendar

Generate a planning calendar from the financial year-end and actual AGM date. The calculator separates Companies Act / MCA obligations from listed-entity SEBI LODR obligations and asks before adding fact-dependent filings.

Core mapping: Section 96 (AGM), Section 92 (annual return), Section 137 (financial statements), Section 173 (Board meetings), Rule 12A (DIR-3 KYC), applicable DPT-3 / MSME Form I / ADT-1 provisions, and current SEBI LODR / stock-exchange compliance calendars for listed entities. The output is a planning aid and must be checked against the company's facts and the law in force on the filing date.
MCA filing delay

MCA Late Filing Fee Estimator

Use this as a transparent working estimator. The applicable normal fee and additional-fee rate must be confirmed from the current MCA fee schedule for the selected form.

This tool intentionally keeps the fee rate editable because MCA fee structures, form-specific rules and amendments can change. It does not calculate adjudication/compounding penalties.
Capital table

Shareholding Dilution

Models a primary investment round and optionally a new post-money ESOP pool. It does not model secondary transfers, convertibles or liquidation preferences.

Primary issue calculation: investor ownership before any new ESOP pool = investment ÷ post-money valuation. If an ESOP pool is created post-money, the pool dilutes existing holders and the new investor proportionately.
Financial valuation

DCF Valuation

Five-year unlevered free cash flow model with Gordon Growth terminal value. All projections are user inputs.

Terminal value = Year 5 FCF × (1 + g) ÷ (WACC − g). WACC must exceed terminal growth. This is an illustrative valuation model, not a fairness opinion or valuation certificate.
Foreign investment

FDI / FEMA Reporting Guide

Guided preliminary checklist for common foreign investment reporting events. Sectoral caps, entry route, pricing, beneficial ownership, downstream investment and approvals require fact-specific review.

Key sources: FEMA (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 and FEMA reporting regulations/master directions. Current RBI material states FC-GPR is generally due within 30 days of issue, FLA by 15 July, and FC-TRS within 60 days of transfer or receipt/remittance of funds, whichever is earlier, subject to the precise transaction and applicable framework.
Capital markets

IPO Readiness Checklist

A preliminary management checklist for an equity listing project. It is not a legal eligibility opinion and does not replace due diligence or exchange/SEBI review.

Checklist themes are aligned to the broad governance, financial reporting, disclosure, capital structure and due-diligence framework applicable to public issues/listing. Eligibility and disclosure requirements must be checked against the current SEBI ICDR, LODR and applicable stock exchange requirements.
