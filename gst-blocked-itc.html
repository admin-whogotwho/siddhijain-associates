Section 17(5) of the CGST Act

Blocked ITC is category-specific and exception-driven.

Section 17(5) restricts input tax credit for specified categories of goods and services. The restriction is not a substitute for analysing the actual use of the supply, the statutory exceptions and the precise wording applicable to the transaction.

This decision aid identifies the common provision associated with the selected category. It does not conclude whether a particular invoice is eligible for credit. That conclusion requires a review of the facts, business use, outward supply, statutory obligations and applicable exceptions.

Common review points

Questions to ask before reversing or claiming ITC

What was purchased?

Identify the exact goods or services and the relevant clause of section 17(5).

How is it used?

Determine whether the inward supply is used for business, taxable outward supply or a specified purpose covered by an exception.

Is there an exception?

Several blocked-credit clauses contain specific exceptions. The exception must be tested against the facts.

Plant and machinery

For construction-related restrictions, review the statutory meaning and the treatment of plant and machinery carefully.

CSR expenditure

Section 17(5)(fa) addresses goods or services used or intended for activities relating to corporate social responsibility obligations under section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Tax period

Confirm the law applicable to the relevant tax period, including amendments and their effective dates.
