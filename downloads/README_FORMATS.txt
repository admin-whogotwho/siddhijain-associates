SIDDHI JAIN & ASSOCIATES – PROFESSIONAL WORKING FORMATS

The resource set contains 28 Word working formats: 10 Board resolutions, 10 Board minutes formats and 8 listed-company Board outcome formats. The documents use a plain black/grey professional layout with larger text, generous spacing, structured tables and drafting controls. They are not prescribed statutory forms. Verify current law, SS-1/SS-2, SEBI/RBI requirements, stock-exchange circulars and transaction-specific facts before use.
