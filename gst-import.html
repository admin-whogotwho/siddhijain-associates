GST · Imports

Import GST
Calculator

Estimate the customs-side tax build-up for imported goods using an assessable value, BCD, SWS and IGST assumptions.

Formula: BCD = assessable value × BCD rate. SWS is calculated on BCD as entered. IGST base is assessable value + BCD + SWS + other duty entered. Actual customs assessment can depend on valuation rules, exemptions, tariff notifications, anti-dumping/safeguard duties, IGST exemptions and other applicable provisions.
How the calculator works

Estimate the import tax build-up before customs assessment.

The calculator starts with the assessable value and applies the BCD, Social Welfare Surcharge and IGST assumptions entered above. It is intended for preliminary estimation and planning, not for determining the final customs liability.

For imported goods, customs valuation is governed by the applicable valuation provisions and rules. The actual duty computation can be affected by tariff classification, exemptions, preferential treatment, valuation adjustments, anti-dumping or safeguard duties, cess, and other notifications or orders applicable to the goods.

Important checks

What should be reviewed before relying on the result?

Assessable value

Confirm the customs value under the applicable valuation rules rather than relying only on the supplier invoice.

Tariff classification

BCD and other customs treatment depend on the correct tariff heading and applicable notifications.

IGST base

Imported-goods IGST is determined under the Customs Tariff framework and may depend on the duties and value relevant to the import.

Exemptions

Check exemption notifications, concessional rates and conditions before finalising the payable amount.

Special duties

Anti-dumping, safeguard or other applicable duties may change the overall customs outgo.

Professional review

Use the result as an estimate and verify the Bill of Entry, tariff treatment and current notifications for the actual transaction.
